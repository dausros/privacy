Daus Automations – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Daus Automations ("we") builds WhatsApp and chat automations for businesses in Singapore. This policy explains how we handle information when you message a WhatsApp number run by us or for one of our clients.

What we collect

Your WhatsApp number and profile name, and the messages you send, including details you choose to share such as your name, address, preferred dates and the service you need.

How we use it

Only to reply to you, to arrange the service you asked about, and to pass your enquiry to the business you contacted. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Messages are processed by Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and by our own systems. We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to handle your request, and no longer than 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). Just message us or email the address below.
